About

For 17 years, Justin Suits has brought stories to life with a voice that's deep, smooth, and endlessly versatile. From audiobooks to video games and beyond, he's lent his range to characters of every kind — heroes, villains, narrators, and everyone in between.

What drives him isn't just the performance, but the story itself, and finding the exact tone it deserves. When he's not behind the mic, you'll find him chasing that same love of language in other forms.
